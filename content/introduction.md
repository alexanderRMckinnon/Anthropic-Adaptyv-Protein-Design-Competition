I took part in the **2026 Anthropic × Adaptyv Protein Design Competition**, submitting designs for three challenges: a conditional EGFR binder, a GLP-1 receptor agonist, and a mutation-specific KRAS peptide–HLA binder.

The competition brings computational protein design together with experimental testing of selected submissions. My part was to propose candidate sequences and build a case for why they were worth testing.

This is my account of that work: the approaches I tried, the decisions I made, and the questions I was left with. The outline is here, and I’m adding the detailed methods and reflections as I write them up.
