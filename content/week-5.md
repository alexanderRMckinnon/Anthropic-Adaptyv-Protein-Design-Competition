### The challenge

Recognise a KRAS G12D peptide presented by HLA-A*11:01 while avoiding the wild-type peptide and the peptide-deleted HLA complex.

### My starting approach

I generated a de novo binder family and developed it through partial diffusion and targeted sequence changes. I compared the designs against mutant, wild-type and peptide-deleted target contexts.

A recurring difficulty was that a convincing mutant interface could also accommodate wild type. That became an important part of how I interpreted the predictions and chose my submission.
